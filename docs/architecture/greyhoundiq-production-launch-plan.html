04

Known residual risk

These are accepted launch constraints, not hidden claims of full dual-region readiness.

Single region

A Sydney regional outage can interrupt the service until the Melbourne cell and failover path are proven.

Edge quota

The project Cloud Armor security-policy quota is currently zero; origin-grade WAF protection is blocked on Google approval.

Credit continuity

Free-trial credits are the current commercial footing. Billing continuity and cost alerts must be established before the trial boundary.

Interim realtime

The support database and self-hosted Supabase Realtime remain until the outbox, Pub/Sub and WebSocket replacement is live and verified.

Scale evidence

Dual-region load, failover, storage recovery, LiveKit N+1 and full disaster-recovery gates remain unverified.

05

What “one database” means

One source of business truth does not mean deleting infrastructure-owned system databases or an interim support store before its service is replaced.

Authoritative application truth

giq_production_stage11_20260718_r2 is the only GreyhoundIQ application database. Accounts, profiles, racing data, feed state and other business records live here. No second application database is populated or kept as a competing source.

Not competing business databases

  • AlloyDB/PostgreSQL system databases are platform-owned and retained.
  • giq_realtime_stage11 is an interim operational support store.
  • The obsolete rehearsal restore database is removed only after launch verification.
  • Stage11 R1 migration or a new legacy database copy.
